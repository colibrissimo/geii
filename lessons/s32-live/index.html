3Check your guesses

Which of your guesses did the video confirm? Which uses did you not expect?

  1. Removing noise. A low-pass filter takes out the cicadas and keeps his voice.
  2. Shock absorbers. Engineers study the vibrations and the resonance when they design them.
  3. 4G and 5G. The phone network uses it to create the signal (OFDM) and to receive it.
  4. Wildlife. Microphones in the forest pick out the sound of a bird or a frog from all the noise.

The sine has only one line...

The square has more lines than the sine, but...

The saw sounds ... and in the picture, it has...

Sine: one line, at 220 Hz. A sine is one frequency, so the picture shows one line.

Square: a ladder with gaps. A line at 220 Hz, then lines at 660, 1100, 1540 Hz... That is 3, 5, 7 times 220: only the odd ones, and each one is quieter than the one below.

Saw: the full ladder. 220, 440, 660, 880 Hz... Every multiple of 220, getting quieter as it goes up. No gaps.

All three sounds play the same note. What changes is the ladder above it, and that is why they sound different.

5Music in a beam of light

The artist Jem Finer sent music across the River Thames, in London, inside a beam of light: from an old lighthouse at Trinity Buoy Wharf to a listening post on the other bank. The music was Longplayer, a piece that lasts 1000 years. The work is called Sonic Ray.

A journalist at the Evening Standard asked: "How do you send a piece of music through a laser beam?" What is your guess?

Read about Sonic Ray

Which is the message, which is the signal, and which is the carrier?

The message: the music.

The carrier: the beam of light. It crosses the river.

The signal: the light with the music encoded in it. On the other bank, a receiver turns it back into sound.

Remember the video: in 4G and 5G, each part of your data is sent on a different carrier.

L'artiste Jem Finer a envoyé de la musique de l'autre côté de la Tamise, à Londres, dans un faisceau de lumière : d'un ancien phare à Trinity Buoy Wharf jusqu'à un poste d'écoute sur l'autre rive. La musique était Longplayer, une œuvre qui dure 1000 ans. L'œuvre s'appelle Sonic Ray.

Un journaliste de l'Evening Standard a demandé : comment envoyer un morceau de musique dans un rayon laser ? Votre hypothèse ?

Open the Spectral Canvas

What came back: the whole shape, now in teal. It is not paint any more, it is a sound.

What did not come back exactly: the sharp edges. They are a little blurred, with a faint glow around them, because the canvas listens to the sound in short slices of time.

Try the eraser on it: nothing happens. It is sound now, so only Clear sound removes it.
