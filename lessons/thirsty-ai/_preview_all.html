What does each verb need behind it?

Before you use the verb, what do you need to have? Proof, a calculation, some signs or data, just confidence, or just a feeling?

From weakest to strongest

a feeling: suspect → confidence: assert, contend → signs or data: indicate, imply, infer → a calculation: estimate → proof: establish

Careful: assert and contend sound strong. They are only as strong as the person’s confidence.

Imply or infer?

The speaker (or the data) implies. The listener infers.

1
The data ___ that prices will rise next year.
2
From the data, we ___ that prices will rise next year.
3
Are you ___ that my calculation is wrong?

Rewrite it

Five more ways data centres affect the people who live near them. Rewrite each sentence with a verb from the box, in the right form. Use a different verb each time.

VERBSassertcontendestablishimplyindicateinfersuspectestimate

According to a report, data centres could increase the typical electricity bill in the US state of Virginia by $37 per month by 2040.

→ It’s been estimated that data centres could increase the typical electricity bill in Virginia by $37 per month by 2040.

A
According to some residents of the areas surrounding data centres, the noise levels reduce their ability to work and sleep.
A possible answer: Some residents of the areas surrounding data centres have indicated that the noise levels reduce their ability to work and sleep.
B
Heavy truck traffic and diesel exhaust emissions are common near data centres, and they will increase further when several data centres are clustered together.
A possible answer: It can be inferred that when several data centres are clustered together, the truck traffic and diesel exhaust emissions will increase.
C
Researchers have found that data centres affect the air and water quality.
A possible answer: It’s been established that data centres affect the air and water quality.
D
People use phrases like ‘uncontrolled growth in AI’ because they don’t think that current regulation is keeping up with data centre expansion.
A possible answer: People use phrases like ‘uncontrolled growth in AI’ to imply that current regulation may not be keeping up with data centre expansion.
E
Some residents worry that farmland will be converted into industrial zones as developers propose more and more data centres.
A possible answer: Some residents suspect that developers will convert farmland into industrial zones as they propose more and more data centres.
