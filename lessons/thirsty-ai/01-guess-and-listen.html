Three numbers, one question

How much water does one message to an AI use? Three different answers.

Who says soWater per messageWhat it countsDid they publish how they calculated it?
Sam Altman, boss of OpenAI (blog, June 2025)about 0.3 mLNot said.No. OpenAI declined to give the BBC details.
Prof. Shaolei Ren, University of California, Riverside (research on a GPT-3-sized model)10 to 50 mLCooling the data centre and producing its electricity.Yes: a research study, Making AI Less ‘Thirsty’.

0.3 mL is 1/15 of a teaspoon. 500 mL for 10 to 50 messages is 10 to 50 mL each.
