How AI uses our drinking water

Watch the next part. Why are these things mentioned? Write a few words for each, then check.

Video: How AI uses our drinking water, BBC World Service (YouTube).

A
air-cooling systems
Most data centres used air cooling, and that was fine until AI came along. (2:06)
B
drinking water
Liquid cooling needs clean water, to stop bacteria, clogs and corrosion. So it is mostly drinking water. (2:24)
C
absorb the heat
The coolant absorbs the heat from the chips and carries it to a heat exchanger. (2:41)
D
80%
Up to 80% of the water evaporates in the cooling towers. (3:10)
E
400%
The International Energy Agency expects electricity demand for AI-optimised data centres to increase by 400% by 2030, to 300 TWh: about the UK’s electricity for a year. (4:00)
F
AI hardware
Water is also needed to make the chips and to refine the raw materials for them. (4:18)
G
Google, Meta and Microsoft annual reports
Their data centres use billions of litres a year from local sources, but none of them say how much of it is due to AI. (4:47)

How strong is each claim?
