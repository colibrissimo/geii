Claim court

Two teams, one screen. Every claim has evidence behind it, or none. Pick the verb the evidence allows.

How to play

A claim appears with a gap. The team on turn picks the verb: 2 points. Wrong? The other team can steal it for 1 point.

The team that gets the verb wins a bonus question, +1: what does that verb need behind it?

On Zoom: the teacher shares the screen and clicks what each team says.

How long?

A random team starts. Both teams get the same number of claims.

The claims you judged

In pairs: pick one claim from the list. Change one detail so that a different verb becomes the right one.

Example: With no evidence yet, residents suspect... → After a year of water tests, it has been established...
