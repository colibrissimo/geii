Your picture, one minute

In pairs. Take turns to talk about your picture for one minute on your own: what it is, what it looks like, what it’s for, whether you have one. Then talk about the questions together.

START LIKE THISIn my picture, there’s a / an...It looks...It’s made of...I think it’s for...

Two or three adjectives, in order: a heavy silver steel watch, an old orange plastic telephone.

Set 1 of 2

1:00

Now together. Talk about the questions.

1Which of the two objects would you like to have? Why?
2Do you pay attention to the design when you buy an everyday object? Why or why not?
