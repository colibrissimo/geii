One change at a time

Change one word at a time to get from the first object to the second. Every step must be a real phrase, with the adjectives in the right order. Watch out for a and an.

from a dirty old window to a clean new car

a dirty old window → a clean old window → a clean new window → a clean new car

2 of 4 · from a soft round pillow to an old cotton towel

4 of 4 · from an old woollen sweater to a huge colourful balloon

All four. Now make one for your partner.
