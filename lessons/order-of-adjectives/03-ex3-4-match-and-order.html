Read each row from left to right. That is the order.

size→quality→age→shape→colour→material→noun

a huge sharp knife · an old wooden clock · a big soft round black woollen hat

Nobody says five adjectives in real life. Two, sometimes three.

Two more places you will see

opinion→size→quality→age→shape→colour→origin→material→noun

a lovely old clock · a Japanese steel knife · a beautiful black Italian leather jacket

A pattern that helps: the closer to the noun, the more the word says what the thing is. A knife can stop being sharp. It can’t stop being steel.
