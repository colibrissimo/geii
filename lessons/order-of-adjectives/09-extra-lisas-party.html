Tom’s funny wardrobe discovery

Tom was looking for his new colourful socks in his messy drawer. He found all sorts of things: a black woollen sweater, an old leather jacket, and even a huge cotton towel, but not his new colourful socks! After a few minutes, he finally found them... on his feet! Tom laughed at himself and decided to organise his drawer.

Lisa was getting ready for a big party and wanted to wear a colourful cotton dress she’d bought especially for the event. But when she put it on, she realised there was a tiny round hole right in the front! She didn’t have time to do anything about it, so she quickly used a huge woollen scarf to make it look better. At the party, everyone looked surprised and kept asking her questions about... her warm scarf. She just smiled and said it was her ‘new style’.
