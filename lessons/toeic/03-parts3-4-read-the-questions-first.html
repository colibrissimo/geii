Parts 3 and 4: read the questions first

In Part 3 you hear a conversation, in Part 4 a talk or a voicemail. Either way: once, then three questions with four options each, which are printed, and you may read them before the audio starts. That is a gift, and most students waste it. Today: one conversation (Part 3). Part 4 works the same way, with one speaker.

FR : Parties 3 et 4 : une conversation (ou un message, une annonce), entendue une seule fois, puis trois questions imprimées. Lisez-les AVANT l'audio. Environ 8 secondes par question.

Step 1. Read the three questions. Do not listen yet. What do you need to catch? Take about 15 seconds.

1. Who most likely is the woman?
2. What problem does the man mention?
3. What will the woman probably do next?

Most of the wrong options reuse a word you heard. Find three (the red words help) and say why each one is still wrong. What does that tell you about the words you recognise?
