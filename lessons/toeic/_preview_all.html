Part 1: the photo

In Part 1 you see a photo and hear four statements, once. Nothing is printed: the photo is all you have. Choose the statement that matches the photo. You get about 5 seconds. Three photos.

FR : Une photo, quatre phrases entendues une seule fois (rien n'est écrit). Choisissez la phrase qui correspond à la photo. Environ 5 secondes pour répondre.

Before you press play: look at the photo. Who is in it? What are they doing? Where? Then listen for the action, not just the objects.

Check your sound first. The audio plays once.

Photos 1 and 3 each had a statement with being. One was right and one was wrong. What decided it? Look at the people in each photo.

Part 2: question and response

In Part 2 you hear a question and three replies, once. Nothing is printed, not even the question. Choose the reply that fits. In the real test you get 5 to 8 seconds; here, 8. Four questions.

FR : Une question, trois réponses, entendues une seule fois (rien n'est écrit). Choisissez la réponse qui convient. 8 secondes ici (5 à 8 au vrai test).

The first word is the whole question. Catch When, Who, Where... and you know what a right answer must be. A reply that repeats a word from the question is almost always bait.

Check your sound first. Each recording plays once.

In the last question the first word was Why. Was the speaker asking for a reason? What was the question really doing, and what kind of reply does that call for?

Parts 3 and 4: read the questions first

In Part 3 you hear a conversation, in Part 4 a talk or a voicemail. Either way: once, then three questions with four options each, which are printed, and you may read them before the audio starts. That is a gift, and most students waste it. Today: one conversation (Part 3). Part 4 works the same way, with one speaker.

FR : Parties 3 et 4 : une conversation (ou un message, une annonce), entendue une seule fois, puis trois questions imprimées. Lisez-les AVANT l'audio. Environ 8 secondes par question.

Step 1. Read the three questions. Do not listen yet. What do you need to catch? Take about 15 seconds.

1. Who most likely is the woman?
2. What problem does the man mention?
3. What will the woman probably do next?

Most of the wrong options reuse a word you heard. Find three (the red words help) and say why each one is still wrong. What does that tell you about the words you recognise?

Part 5: thirty seconds

Part 5 is one sentence with one gap. In the real test there are 30 of them, and the rule is: 30 seconds maximum for each one. You get the same clock here. Six sentences.

FR : Une phrase, un blanc, 30 secondes maximum par phrase. Si le temps est écoulé, la question se verrouille : au vrai test, on répond au jugé et on passe.

Tip: read the four options first. They tell you what is being tested: four forms of one word, four tenses, four prepositions...

Look at the tested column. Which kind of question cost you the most time? That is the one to practise.

Part 6: one text, four gaps

Part 6 is short texts (emails, memos, notices) with four gaps each. Three gaps are a word or a phrase. One gap is a whole sentence. The budget: about 2 minutes for this text.

Part 7: skim, then scan

Part 7 is the long part: 54 questions, about 55 minutes. Reading every word is the fastest way to run out of time. Two moves: skim for the main idea, scan for the detail the question asks.

FR : Survoler pour l'idée générale, puis balayer le texte pour trouver le détail demandé. Ne pas tout lire mot à mot.

1. Skim: 15 seconds

A notice appears for 15 seconds, then disappears. You cannot read it all. Get the main idea.

GreenLine Bikes: notice to customers

From 1 November, our shop on Rue Nationale will move to a larger building at 12 Place Rihour, two minutes from the old address. The new shop will have its own repair workshop, so customers will no longer need to leave their bikes with us for a week. Our opening hours will stay the same.

During the move, from 28 to 31 October, the shop will be closed, but online orders will still be delivered as usual.

What is the notice mainly about?

2. Scan: questions first

An email and a schedule. Read the three questions first, so you know what to look for. Then show the documents: they appear above the questions, as in the real test, and the clock starts. Budget: about 1 minute per question.

FR : Lisez d'abord les trois questions. Ensuite seulement, affichez les documents : le chrono démarre.
