Three words that break the rules

Why are these different? Talk to your partner first, then open each one.

big

We double the last letter: big, bigger, the biggest. Same with hot, hotter, the hottest.

heavy

The -y becomes -i: heavier, the heaviest. Two-syllable -y words can go both ways: healthier or more healthy.

good / bad

They change completely: good, better, the best; bad, worse, the worst.
