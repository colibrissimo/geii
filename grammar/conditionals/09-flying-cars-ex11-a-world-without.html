A world without…

In pairs. One screen each, or take turns on one.

  1. Draw a card and peek at your word. Do not show your screen.
  2. Cover the word, then describe that world using would, could, might, wouldn't.
  3. When your partner guesses, uncover the word to check.

Without them, students wouldn't be so stressed out. But they also might not study much, because some of them would not have any motivation to do that.
