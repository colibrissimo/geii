ZERO

General truths, habits, things that are always true. If I work in a quiet place, I focus much better.
FIRST
If + present simple, will + infinitive
A real possibility in the future. If I work from home next week, I'll ask my family not to enter my room.
SECOND
If + past simple, would + infinitive
Imaginary now or in the future: the opposite of the real situation. If I didn't check my messages so often, I would stay focused for longer.
