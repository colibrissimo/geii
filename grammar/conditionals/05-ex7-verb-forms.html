7 · Put the verb in the correct form

Type the verb in brackets in the form that fits. Look at the other half of the sentence first: it tells you which conditional you need.

Example: People usually get distracted very easily if they are (be) tired or stressed.

A
Students (enjoy) studying more if they had shorter lessons.
B
If everyone (know) the perfect way to stay focused, people wouldn't waste so much time.
C
Teens won't spend so much time scrolling if they (have) something exciting to do offline.
D
If people (practise) focusing every day, their concentration improves.
E
If you try to do several things at once, you (make) more mistakes.
F
You can't remember new information if you (learn) it without understanding it.
G
If you (not take) regular breaks, your brain will get tired more quickly.

Then: choose three sentences. Who would you give this advice to, and why?
