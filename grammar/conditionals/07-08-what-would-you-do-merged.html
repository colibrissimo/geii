What would you do?

In pairs. Three steps, then talk. Do at least three situations.

1. Choose a situation

You have to write a long essay, but you don't know how to begin.

You keep checking your phone every few minutes while you are studying.

You need to concentrate, but your room is noisy.

You have to organise your presentation notes for an important meeting or class.

You often switch between different tasks instead of finishing one.

You find it difficult to stay focused when working alone for several hours, and you keep taking coffee and snack breaks.

2. Which technique would you try?

task breakdown

dividing a big task into smaller, easier steps

the five-minute rule

working on the task for just five minutes, then deciding whether to continue

background music

listening to instrumental music to improve concentration

body doubling

working in the same room as another person who is also working

virtual co-working

working online with others, with regular check-ins
