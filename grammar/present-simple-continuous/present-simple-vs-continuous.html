Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Master English tenses with interactive exercises

Tense Overview

Present Simple

Purpose: Facts, habits, routines, permanent situations

I work here every day.
Water boils at 100 degrees.
She plays tennis on Tuesdays.

Time words: always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day/week/month

Present Continuous

Purpose: Actions happening now, temporary situations, future plans

I'm working right now.
She's living in London for a few weeks.
We're meeting tomorrow at 5pm.

Time words: now, at the moment, currently, these days, tonight, tomorrow

🔑 Key Difference

Present simple gives the big picture: habits, routines, facts and permanent situations.

Present continuous gives a snapshot: actions in progress now, or temporary situations around now.

Forms & Structure

Present Simple - Positive

With 'be': I am / You are / He is

Other verbs: I work / You work / He works

I am a teacher.
She works in London.
Remember: Add 's' to the verb with he/she/it. Special cases: go→goes, do→does, have→has

Present Simple - Negative

With 'be': I'm not / You aren't / He isn't

Other verbs: I don't work / He doesn't work

I'm not hungry.
She doesn't like coffee.
Use 'don't' with I/you/we/they and 'doesn't' with he/she/it. The main verb stays in base form (no 's').

Present Continuous - Positive

Form: am/is/are + verb-ing

I am working now.
She is reading a book.
They are playing football.
Spelling rules: work→working, make→making (drop 'e'), run→running (double consonant), study→studying (keep the y)

Present Continuous - Negative

Form: am/is/are + not + verb-ing

I'm not working today.
She isn't coming to the party.
We aren't watching TV.
Contractions: I'm not, isn't, aren't. The -ing form stays the same in negatives.

Uses & Examples

Present Simple Uses

1. Habits & Routines (A1)

She plays tennis every Tuesday.

2. Permanent Situations (A1)

I live in London. / She's a lawyer.

3. Always True Facts (A1)

Water boils at 100 degrees.

4. Future Timetables (A2)

Our train leaves at 11am.

Present Continuous Uses

1. Actions Happening Now (A1)

I'm having dinner at the moment.

2. Temporary Situations (A2)

I'm living in London for a few weeks.

3. Future Plans (A2)

I'm meeting my father tomorrow.

4. Annoying Habits, with always/forever (B1)

You're forever losing your keys!

Time Expressions

Present Simple:

always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day/week, on Mondays, twice a month

Present Continuous:

now, right now, at the moment, currently, these days, tonight, tomorrow, next week

Common Mistakes

❌ I am work here.

✅ I work here.

❌ She working now.

✅ She is working now.

❌ I'm knowing the answer.

✅ I know the answer.

*State verbs don't use continuous
