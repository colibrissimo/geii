**Status: built 18 Aug 2026. Audio verified. Not yet on Moodle.**

Self-contained static deck, same shell as `short-long-i`. No account, no
subscription, works offline from a USB stick. Open `index.html`.

## Decisions taken 18 Aug 2026
- **The quiz is pair discrimination, not aspirated/unaspirated classification.**
  A "was there a puff?" quiz over all 36 words would have an arguable key:
  *police, computer, potential, capacity* carry the plosive in an **unstressed**
  syllable, where aspiration is weak or absent. Those four are on the Notes
  slide as the stated exception instead, and the quiz stays on the 8 pairs
  where the answer is never debatable.
- **`battery` is used as the p/b illustration**, not left out as the odd word.
  It is the only /b/ in the folder and it makes the real point: English separates
  p from b by aspiration, not by voicing.
- **No `assets/img`, no video.** Nothing was available in the folder. The deck
  uses CSS instead — an animated three-ring "puff" on the title, oversize
  letterforms on the section slides, the dot fields from the shared shell.
- **The quiz play button is a CSS disc, not the cassette image.** `yellow_tape.png`
  lives in `short-long-i/assets/img/` and was not copied; duplicating it across
  decks would mean maintaining two copies of a file Sarah has already de-branded once.

## Audio provenance
All 36 recordings were made by Sarah on **TTSmp3.com** ("read", then download,
each saved under its own word as filename), and checked by ear at the point of
creation. Not `say`-generated — do not assume the offline pipeline here.
