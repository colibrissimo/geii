Meet /h/

There is no tongue position, no lips, no vibration. You simply push air out before the vowel — like fogging a window.

French has no /h/ at all, so the muscle is not missing, it is unused.

Put your hand in front of your mouth and say house. You should feel warmth.

The invented H

The mirror-image mistake. Having learned that English likes its H, French speakers start putting one where there is none: h-energy, h-idea, h-apple.

These seven begin on a bare vowel. Open your mouth and let the vowel arrive with no breath in front of it.
