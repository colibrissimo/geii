TECHNOLOGY PHRASAL VERBS

The 30-verb deck · BUT2 GEII · one small verb + one small particle = one precise meaning

Why engineers should care: English builds these verbs the way you build circuits, from a small set of standard components. And in tech vocabulary the verbs came FIRST: back up became a backup, log in became a login, set up became the setup, plug in became a plugin. You type these nouns every day. They are fossilised verbs.
SEP separable: the pronoun goes INSIDE (back it up, never back up it) INSEP inseparable: nothing goes inside (run out of it) NOUN this verb produced a noun you already use

1 · Start the day (the eternal order of the bench)

hook up SEP
connect cables between machines or to a supply Hook up the probe before you touch anything else. / Hook it up to the mains.
power up SEP
give power to a machine (opposite: power down) The oscilloscope won't power up: check the fuse first, then panic.
turn on / turn off SEP
activate / stop a device Turn it off and turn it back on: forty years of IT support in one sentence.
boot up / start up INSEP
(of a system) start running Wait, the PLC is still booting up.
log in / log out INSEP NOUN: a login
enter / leave a session with your credentials (also: sign in / sign out) Log in to the workstation. The first verb ever sent over the Internet, in 1969, was log in.

2 · Connect and install

plug in SEP NOUN: a plugin
insert a cord into an outlet or port The laptop is plugged in but it won't charge. Plug it in properly.
set up SEP NOUN: the setup
install and configure a device or program Set up the new scope on the shared network, then set it up for the next group too.
sign up INSEP
register, create an account Sign up for the lab access list before Friday.
key in SEP
type characters, enter data by keyboard Key in the calibration values, then check them twice.
sync up SEP
make two systems hold the same data or time The datalogger and the PC need to sync up before the measurement run.
roll out SEP NOUN: a rollout
deploy something new to many users or machines IT will roll out the update to every lab machine over the weekend.

3 · Work

click on INSEP
select with the mouse or a tap Never click on a link you do not trust. Never.
scroll up / scroll down INSEP
move up or down through a page (also: scroll through) Scroll down to page 47 of the datasheet. Yes, 47.
zoom in / zoom out INSEP
magnify / widen the view Zoom in on the waveform: that spike is your problem.
print out SEP NOUN: a printout
send a document to the printer Print out the wiring diagram, or print it out for the whole team.
run through INSEP
check or rehearse from start to finish Run through the checklist before you close the cabinet.
figure out SEP
find the solution by thinking We could not figure out why it failed, so we slept on it and figured it out at breakfast.
come up with INSEP
invent, produce an idea Dr. Dave Bradley came up with a three-key sequence you could not hit by mistake.

4 · When it breaks (it will)

go down INSEP
stop working (systems, servers, networks) The server went down during the storm. So did our weekend.
crash / freeze + start over INSEP
start over = begin again from zero Hit Ctrl+Alt+Delete and start over: you delete everything you are working on right now, and start new.
wipe out SEP
erase completely The power cut wiped out the unsaved data. Which brings us to the next verb.
back up SEP NOUN: a backup
copy files somewhere safe Back up the firmware before you flash it. Back it up now. There are two kinds of engineers: those who back up, and those who will.
run out of INSEP
exhaust a supply We have run out of disk space, solder wire and excuses.

5 · Protect yourself

hack into INSEP
enter a system illegally Someone hacked into the university network. The particle carries the direction: you break INTO a place.
pop up INSEP NOUN: a popup
appear suddenly on screen A warning popped up and everyone clicked it away without reading it.
filter out SEP
remove what you do not want Filter out the spam, keep the datasheets.
opt in / opt out INSEP
choose to receive / refuse something Always opt out of the marketing emails. Always.

6 · End the day

shut down / power down SEP
close the system, remove power Shut down the workstation. Shut it down properly, not the button-held-for-five-seconds way.
put away SEP
return things to their storage place Unplug the cables and put them away. The lab technician will know if you did not.
go online / go offline INSEP
connect to / disconnect from the Internet The gateway goes offline every night at 2 a.m. So should you.
turn up / turn down SEP
increase / decrease volume or intensity Turn down the gain. Turn it down. TURN IT DOWN.
The one rule worth memorising With a SEPARABLE verb, a pronoun MUST go inside: back it up, set it up, turn it off. "Back up it" is the single most common error in your future emails, and now you will never make it. With an INSEPARABLE verb the words never split: run out of it, hack into it, click on it.
