Connect Four

Internet of Things · 30 words

Two players, one screen. Get four discs in a row: across, up and down, or diagonally.

To earn a disc, translate a French word: pick the English from four. Right: drop your disc in any column. Wrong: no disc, and it is the other player's turn.

Block your opponent and plan your own lines. The game picks who starts. If the starting player connects four, the other player still gets one last turn: if they connect four too, it is a draw.

Keys: 1 2 3 4 to answer, 1 to 7 to drop a disc, Enter for the next turn.
